History of Pitt Sailbot

Pitt Sailbot was founded in 2015 by a group of students at the University of Pittsburgh. The team was created with the goal of designing and building an autonomous sailboat that could compete in the International Robotic Sailing Competition (IRSC). Since then, the team has grown and evolved, with new members joining each year to contribute their skills and ideas. Over the years, Pitt Sailbot has participated in several competitions and has achieved notable success, including winning the 2018 IRSC competition in the Netherlands. The team continues to innovate and push the boundaries of autonomous sailing technology, with a focus on developing cutting-edge software and hardware solutions for their sailboat.
